# Quick Start

# Overview

In WHALE, a client bank card refers to a bank account opened by the user at a bank, which is bound by the user themselves or by back office personnel on their behalf. The corresponding bank card can be used for deposits and withdrawals in the broker app.

1. Clients add a bank card in the app: The card will be displayed automatically on the page.

<img src="/assets/JW2PbUPuloOvgdxoHeijzqqZp3g.png" src-width="2982" src-height="1290" align="center"/>

1. When clients cannot bind a card in the client app: In such cases, a back office operator can add the bank card in the back office. After addition, it will be displayed automatically on the page.
    Click Add bank cards to enter the page, fill in the detailed information, and submit.

<img src="/assets/UUyUbmY7goKxm5xIXiij0ZJypQb.png" src-width="2980" src-height="746" align="center"/>

<img src="/assets/XPsbbzzukoJxHtx8H0yjgKWZpqf.png" src-width="2450" src-height="3123" align="center"/>

Click Batch Add to add bank card information in batches according to the template

<img src="/assets/FWDNbW2doo8tcuxOxjojrMOVpqf.png" src-width="3002" src-height="772" align="center"/>

<img src="/assets/IvnDbZNwJoRV2AxhVIBjNs4zpng.png" src-width="3026" src-height="1334" align="center"/>

1. For client-submitted information requiring manual review, or for cards added manually in the back office on behalf of clients, when the bank card authentication status is Authentication In Progress, manual review is required.

<img src="/assets/AJ0VbGUCUo4SvuxsJbljOPcrpTT.png" src-width="2978" src-height="1316" align="center"/>

When a client's bank card authentication status is Without Witness or Have Witnessed, the back office operator can modify the bank card information.

<img src="/assets/JocmbNZDVoPRaTxu1Z5juGvipWg.png" src-width="2986" src-height="1268" align="center"/>

<img src="/assets/ZCSFbQMV9oRSuexWrpajdnOLpbc.png" src-width="2400" src-height="3080" align="center"/>

1. Manually updating the fund deposit witness status: 
    If a client's fund deposit transaction is completed, but their bank card's witness status remains To Witness, operators can click Update Deposit Verification to handle it.

<img src="/assets/JoKvbejRQoWJ5LxWB8IjCvBLpef.png" src-width="2970" src-height="1306" align="center"/>

After linking the client's deposit details, the system will automatically update the fund deposit witness status to Have Witnessed.
