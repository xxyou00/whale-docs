# System Overview

# I. System Overview

Bank cards are used in the processes of withdrawals, deposits, and company bank statement reconciliation. This feature provides basic information configuration. The main related modules involved are as follows:

<img src="/assets/YOzXwpsBGhE9FwbMtI0jujOkpog-board.jpeg"/>

# II. Operation Instructions

Whether creating a bank card in the app or in the back office (WBO), it is necessary to first configure the card's attributes, such as the region where the bank card is issued and the issuing bank.

1. Maintain the card’s issuing bank (known bank information, including bank name, bank_code, swift_code, bank address, and related icons)

<div class="callout callout-bg-5 callout-border-5 callout-color-1">
<div class='callout-emoji'>📍</div>
<p> Navigation: Cash Management &gt; App Config &gt; Supported Bank Setups</p>
</div>

- Create: Click Create to enter the page and fill in the required information.
    After completion, you may also click Edit in the Action column to modify the corresponding fields.

<img src="/assets/QnMebHcf6oKwDTxWbPQjDLdrpKg.png" src-width="3806" src-height="1744" align="center"/>

<img src="/assets/FXMkbwdK9o1g5NxiFddjK92VpSd.png" src-width="2440" src-height="1834" align="center"/>

1. Confirm the country/region of the bank card

<div class="callout callout-bg-5 callout-border-5 callout-color-1">
<div class='callout-emoji'>📍</div>
<p>Navigation: Cash Management &gt; App Config &gt; Bank Card - Country/Region</p>
</div>

- Ensure that Whether Card Binding Is Allowed is set to Allow.

<img src="/assets/WhYgbfTbNoIJO5xqUwhj0Vy1pWb.png" src-width="2984" src-height="1306" align="center"/>

After completing the attribute configuration for the card, the operator can proceed with bank card management.

<div class="callout callout-bg-5 callout-border-5 callout-color-1">
<div class='callout-emoji'>📍</div>
<p>Navigation: Cash Management &gt; Bank Card Management</p>
</div>

1. Clients add a bank card in the app: The card will be displayed automatically on the page.

<img src="/assets/SZYoblv5LoKuTlxLu5Nj2jsNpFg.png" src-width="3004" src-height="1306" align="center"/>

1. When clients cannot bind a card in the client app: In such cases, a back office operator can add the bank card in the back office. After addition, it will be displayed automatically on the page.

- Click Add bank cards to enter the page, fill in the detailed information, and submit.

<img src="/assets/Iw9wbQ2okojPfzx4pM5jW1d0pSh.png" src-width="1280" src-height="320" align="center"/>

<img src="/assets/KIGIbYS4NoUKGtxN8EgjBvqapkd.png" src-width="1004" src-height="1280" align="center"/>

- Click Batch Add to add bank card information in batches according to the template

<img src="/assets/AuIXbotIYoNcBTxrgmWjn3zwpKg.png" src-width="1280" src-height="329" align="center"/>

<img src="/assets/FuY2bXjnMo7SIgx83BGjbxGtpog.png" src-width="1280" src-height="564" align="center"/>

1. For client-submitted information requiring manual review, or for cards added manually in the back office on behalf of clients:

- When the bank card authentication status is Authentication In Progress, manual review is required.

<img src="/assets/H8KrbzodPofuEPxnEIHjd4IppUd.png" src-width="1280" src-height="565" align="center"/>

- When a client's bank card authentication status is Without Witness or Have Witnessed, the back office operator can modify the bank card information.

<img src="/assets/Nf8qbEMLFoO9zjxUon7jP0cjpkb.png" src-width="1280" src-height="543" align="center"/>

<img src="/assets/YpOkbhRN5oRMGtxBSoijv7RppXb.png" src-width="997" src-height="1280" align="center"/>

1. Manually updating the fund deposit witness status:

- If a client's fund deposit transaction is completed, but their bank card's witness status remains To Witness, operators can click Update Deposit Verification to handle it.

<img src="/assets/MhyqbjcFgotmMvxIQb3jnl04pnd.png" src-width="1280" src-height="562" align="center"/>

- After linking the client's deposit details (or even without linking to a specific deposit record), the system will automatically update the fund deposit witness status to Have Witnessed.

<img src="/assets/YvE8bFdvyoCPXaxU9hkj0Q8dpAh.png" src-width="1280" src-height="354" align="center"/>

1. To add remarks for a client's bank card, click Remark in the Action column.

<img src="/assets/V61gbkWoeoueA2xabuJjPtv8p1t.png" src-width="2970" src-height="1292" align="center"/>

<img src="/assets/C8lYbXdWHoB3X1xDM93j627ipxd.png" src-width="3075" src-height="1388" align="center"/>

1. Deleting a client's bank card:

- Click Delete in the back office to delete it.

<img src="/assets/LBcPbaeIvoFCeaxyzfDjNp0SpLg.png" src-width="2980" src-height="1280" align="center"/>

- Click Recycle Bin to view deleted data.

<img src="/assets/NgNSbWEkdotReNxVN98jQKLcpEa.png" src-width="2988" src-height="1336" align="center"/>
